\section*{Problem 1.1}
The sequence problem of the Cass-Koopmans model is given by 
\begin{align*}
    & V^*(k_0) = \max_{\{k_{t+1}\}_0^\infty} \sum_{t=0}^\infty \beta^t \frac{c_t^{1 - \gamma} - 1}{1 - \gamma} \\
    \text{subject to } \quad & k_{t+1} = (1 - \delta) k_t + A k_t^\alpha - c_t \\
    & k_0 \text{ is given }
\end{align*}
After substituting the resource constraints, we have 
\[
 V^*(k_0) = \max_{\{k_{t+1}\}_0^\infty} \sum_{t=0}^\infty \beta^t \frac{((1 - \delta) k_t + A k_t^\alpha - k_{t+1})^{1 - \gamma} - 1}{1 - \gamma}
\]
Take the derivative w.r.t. $k_{t+1}$, we have 
\[
\beta^t \bigg[ (1-\delta)k_t + Ak_t^\alpha - k_{t+1} \bigg]^{-\gamma} (-1) + \beta^{t+1} 
 \bigg[ (1-\delta)k_{t+1} + Ak_{t+1}^\alpha - k_{t+2} \bigg]^{-\gamma} \bigg(1 - \delta + A \alpha k_{t+1}^{\alpha - 1}\bigg) = 0
\]
After simplification, we have 
\[
\bigg[ (1-\delta)k_t + Ak_t^\alpha - k_{t+1} \bigg]^{-\gamma} = \beta
 \bigg[ (1-\delta)k_{t+1} + Ak_{t+1}^\alpha - k_{t+2} \bigg]^{-\gamma} \bigg(1 - \delta + A \alpha k_{t+1}^{\alpha - 1}\bigg)
\]

In the steady state, $k_t = k_{t+1} = k_{t+2} = \bar{k}$, as a result, we can compute the ss $\bar{k}$ as follows.
\[
\bar{k} = \left(\frac{1 - \beta + \delta \beta}{A \alpha \beta} \right)^{\frac{1}{\alpha - 1}}
\]

After substituting $c_t$ back to the Euler equation, we have 
\[
c_t^{-\gamma} = \beta (1 - \delta + A \alpha k_{t+1}^{\alpha - 1}) c_{t+1}^{-\gamma}
\]
The equation above implies the behavior of consumption smoothing, 
where the agent equalizes the marginal utility of spending today (LHS) and 
the marginal utility of spending tomorrow (RHS).

\end{align*}
